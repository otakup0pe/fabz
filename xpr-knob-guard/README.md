# XPR (and more) series "Knob Guard"

The OpenSCAD file was created with some assistance from a cursed computer hallucination during the pre-season of Burning Man 2025. The resulting device may be used for protect the volume and channel knobs on a variety of [MOTOTRBO](https://wiki.radioreference.com/index.php/MOTOTRBO) series radios. Many users report the volume knobs changing unexpectedly when entering/exiting vehicles, doing complicated physical tasks while wearing radio harnesses, or dropping your radio in a cooler.

There are two included STL files for the XPR 7550, which account for the (slightly) different antenna I have stumbled across. There is a single XPR 3500 and R7 file. There are a wide variety of parameters which may be set, including

* Layout parameters which may support different XPR radios.
* Configurable coverage and (radial) placement of the volume and channel knob guards.
* An optional "notch" to make it easier to detach/attach a shoulder mic.
* Optional labels on the sides or bottom.
